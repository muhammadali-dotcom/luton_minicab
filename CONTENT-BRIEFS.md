# Luton Minicab: inner page content briefs

Briefs for the pages that are still **holding pages**: Services, Our Fleet, Areas, About and Contact. Approve a brief before its page is written. Every fact must come from [requirement.md](requirement.md); anything marked **Needed** must be supplied first. Page titles and descriptions are in [SEO.md](SEO.md); style rules are in [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md).

**Rules for all pages:**
- Simple, warm British English.
- Short paragraphs.
- No superlatives or unverified claims.
- One h1, then sections that alternate light and dark cream.
- Every page ends with the booking banner ("Ready when you are.") and Book Online in the same tab.

## 1. Services (`/services`)
**Purpose:** explain each service clearly enough that a passenger knows it fits their journey, then send them to book. **Live** (November 2026): overview plus five service pages; add details here as the owner confirms them.

**Sections:**
1. **Intro:** one or two lines on journeys for every part of the day, from Luton and beyond.
2. **Local journeys:** everyday trips around Luton and the surrounding areas.
3. **Airport transfers:** all UK airports, flight tracking, meet and greet.
4. **School runs:** regular school journeys with DBS-checked drivers.
5. **SEND & assisted transport:** approved wording only, "For passengers who need a little extra support. Tell us about any needs when you book." No mention of wheelchairs, escorts or medical care.
6. **Corporate & business travel:** journeys to meetings, offices and events. **Business travel is the top commercial priority**, so give it a strong section. No accounts or monthly invoicing (decided): businesses book and pay per journey (cash or online payment link).
7. **Long-distance journeys:** longer trips beyond Luton.
8. **Group journeys:** larger cars and MPVs, subject to availability. Never call them minibuses.
9. **How to book:** online, by phone or by WhatsApp. School runs and SEND use the same channels.

**Can use:** fixed fares, flight tracking, meet and greet, child seats on request, DBS-checked drivers, 24/7, cash or online payment link.

**Needed:**
- Any service details you want added, for example how regular school runs are arranged, and whether long-distance trips have a typical range.
- Should corporate travel mention anything about how businesses book?
